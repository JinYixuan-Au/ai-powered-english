Senior High English — AI Learning Journey

Project Goal

Build a cinematic, modern, interactive Angular website for the first English lesson of senior high school.

The website should help students:

1. Feel welcomed into senior high school.
2. Understand that English is a language that connects them to the world.
3. Understand the key differences between junior high and senior high English learning.
4. Learn effective senior high English learning methods.
5. Experience how AI can support learning without replacing thinking.

The website is designed primarily for classroom projection, but should also work well on laptops and phones.

Narrative Structure

Section 1 — Opening

Theme: Welcome to Jiaotong University Affiliated High School.

Purpose:
Create a strong sense of entering a new stage of life.

Core copy:

* Your journey starts here.
* Welcome to Senior High School.
* Welcome to a new way of learning English.

Visual direction:

* Cinematic
* Minimal
* Elegant
* Strong sense of arrival
* Subtle school-related visual elements
* Large typography
* Smooth transitions

Do not make it look like a school administration website.

Section 2 — English & The World

Main question:

“What can English take you to?”

Purpose:
Show students that English is not only an exam subject. It connects them to places, people, cultures, knowledge and opportunities around the world.

Core idea:
Places encountered in English textbooks can become places students may one day experience themselves.

Possible ending copy:

* English connects you to a bigger world.
* A place in your textbook today may become a place under your feet tomorrow.
* The world is closer than you think.

Visual direction:
Use a world-map-inspired visual journey with animated locations and smooth scroll-based transitions.

Section 3 — Junior High → Senior High

Explain changes in learning mindset, not simply increased difficulty.

Possible themes:

* Answer → Evidence
* Memorizing → Understanding in context
* Correct → Effective
* Correction → Reflection

Section 4 — How to Learn

Senior high English learning methods.

Possible framework:

* Read
* Notice
* Use
* Reflect

This section may be refined later.

Section 5 — Enter the AI Era

Narrative transition:
Students today have a new learning tool that previous generations did not have.

Core message:
AI is not an answer machine.
AI can be a learning partner.

Possible roles:

* Explain
* Challenge
* Feedback
* Reflect

Section 6 — AI English Lab

A real AI-powered interaction.

The exact activity will be designed later.

The AI should encourage student thinking instead of directly completing work for them.

Section 7 — Ending

Return visually to the school / starting point.

Possible closing:

* Three years from now, where will English take you?
* Start here. See the world. Find your voice.
* Welcome to Senior High English.

Visual Style

The experience should feel:

* Modern
* Premium
* Editorial
* Cinematic
* Youthful but not childish
* Calm rather than flashy

Reference feeling:
Apple-style storytelling and scrolling, but not a direct imitation.

Avoid:

* Cartoon school graphics
* Generic education website layouts
* Excessive gradients
* Too many cards
* Dashboard-like UI
* Busy navigation
* Overuse of animations

Frontend Principles

* Angular
* SCSS
* Full-screen storytelling sections
* Responsive design
* Smooth scrolling
* Scroll-triggered transitions
* Subtle motion
* Strong typography hierarchy
* Reusable components where appropriate

Prioritize storytelling and visual continuity over feature density.

Development Approach

Build incrementally.

Do NOT build all sections at once.

First establish:

1. Global visual language
2. Opening section
3. Transition into the world section

After the visual direction is approved, continue to later sections.

Do not add backend or AI API integration yet.

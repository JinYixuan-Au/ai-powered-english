04 / How to learn

So, how should
you learn?

01

Read

Read beyond the question.

Read to understand ideas, not only to finish exercises.

02

Notice

Notice how English works.

Pay attention to useful expressions, structures, tone, and context.

03

Use

Turn what you know into what you can use.

Use language actively—in speaking, writing, and explanation.

04

Reflect

Understand why you were wrong.

Do not only record the answer. Ask: “Why did I choose the wrong one?”
